Variables & Dependencies

Every input, anchor and derived quantity in the Alaska Airlines decarbonization model, and the order in which they depend on each other.

Engine: src/model/ · Config: config/assumptions.yaml

Reading this. The model is deterministic and has no solver, no optimizer and no feedback loops. It runs strictly forward: physical scale first, then SAF, then prices, then money. Anything marked new was added during the port and does not come from Alaska_Airlines_Model_Revenue_Anchored_v3.xlsx.

4. The dependency chain

One pass per year, 2025–2040, in this order. Nothing later feeds anything earlier.

1  physical_year()

activity ← FAA index × (1+activity_adj)n · efficiency ← EIA index × (1+efficiency_adj)n · fleet and propulsion ramp linearly to their 2040 endpoints.
→ liquid_fuel_gal = base_fuel × activity / efficiency × fleet_factor × (1 − propulsion)

2  revenue

revenue_2025 × activity_index. Revenue tracks activity and nothing else — no yields, no fares, no fuel-price pass-through.

3  catalytic_capacity()

invest = revenue × catalytic_pct, each year. Matures after 3 years into extra capacity at $14.67/gal. Cumulative spend is tracked separately for step 6.

4  SAF supply gate

target_saf_share (piecewise-linear 2025→2030→2040) sets target_saf_gal. US supply comes from the published anchors plus catalytic capacity.
effective_saf_share = min(target, available / liquid_fuel) — the only place a constraint binds anywhere in the model.

5  intensity_and_emissions()

carbon_mix = 1 − effective_saf_share × 0.80, normalised by the 2025 mix.
intensity = intensity_2025 / efficiency × fleet_factor × (carbon_mix / baseline_mix) × (1 − propulsion)
→ reduction_vs_2019, residual_emis (aircraft + vehicle + facility + Scope 2)

6  the two prices new

SAF premium = market × (1 − offtake share × (1 − offtake ratio)) — volume Alaska’s capital funded prices cost-plus, the rest pays spot. The discount is bounded by how much of its own fuel it funded and never reaches fuel it did not.
carbon price = base × (1 + escalation)t−2025 — rises with time.
Both return their identity at zero investment / zero escalation, which is what holds parity.

7  money

net_saf_premium from fuel_costs() · offset_cost = residual × carbon price · saf_cost_per_tonne = jet × premium × (1 − partner) / 0.007336
→ annual_cost = net_saf_premium + catalytic_investment + offset_cost

What depends on what, in one line. Activity and efficiency set fuel burn. Fuel burn and SAF share set emissions. Emissions and the carbon price set the offset bill. Cumulative investment sets the SAF price. Nothing sets activity except the FAA baseline and one slider.
